# RFM Customer Segmentation & VIP Analysis Report

## Project Overview

**Project:** E-Commerce Customer Retention & Sales Performance Analytics  
**Assignment:** Build an RFM Customer Segmentation Model to Identify Top VIP Accounts  
**Tools:** Python, pandas, matplotlib, VS Code, GitHub  
**Dataset:** Online Retail Transactions  
**Analysis Period:** December 1, 2010 – December 9, 2011

## 1. Objective

The objective of this analysis is to develop an RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) segmentation model to evaluate customer purchasing behavior, identify high-value VIP customers, and provide actionable recommendations for improving customer retention and revenue performance.

## 2. Methodology

The cleaned retail transaction dataset was analyzed using Python and pandas.

The following metrics were calculated:

- **Recency:** Number of days since the customer's most recent purchase.
- **Frequency:** Number of distinct invoices associated with each customer.
- **Monetary:** Total positive transaction value generated by each customer.

Each customer was assigned RFM scores ranging from 1 to 5 using percentile-based ranking.

VIP customers were defined as those receiving scores of at least 4 for Recency, Frequency, and Monetary.

Customers were classified into four segments: VIP Customers, Loyal Customers, Regular Customers, and At Risk.

The model excludes missing customer identifiers, cancelled invoices, and transactions with nonpositive quantities or prices.

## 5. Key Business Insights

### VIP Customer Concentration

VIP customers represent 21.72% of the customer base but generate 64.56% of analyzed revenue. This indicates that a relatively small group of high-value customers contributes the majority of sales.

### Customer Loyalty

High-frequency customers demonstrate recurring purchasing behavior and may be strong candidates for loyalty programs, personalized offers, and account management initiatives.

### Customer Retention Opportunities

Customers with low recency scores can be targeted through re-engagement campaigns, while VIP customers may benefit from proactive retention efforts.

### Revenue Optimization

RFM segmentation enables the business to prioritize marketing and customer relationship management activities based on observed purchasing behavior.

## 6. Business Recommendations

1. Introduce personalized loyalty rewards and exclusive offers for VIP customers.
2. Prioritize high-value accounts for proactive customer support and relationship management.
3. Develop re-engagement campaigns for customers showing prolonged purchasing inactivity.
4. Use RFM segments to support targeted promotions and customer lifetime value analysis.
5. Integrate customer segments into Power BI dashboards for ongoing performance monitoring.

## 7. Model Limitations

- RFM scores are based on historical transactions and do not guarantee future customer behavior.
- Percentile ranking may assign different scores to customers with identical metric values.
- Revenue excludes missing customer IDs, cancelled invoices, and nonpositive transactions.
- Monetary values are based on positive sales and do not represent net revenue after returns.
- Segment definitions are business rules that may require refinement based on future analysis.
