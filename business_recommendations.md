# Business Analysis Recommendations
**Project:** E-Commerce Customer Retention & Sales Performance Analytics

## 1. Business Overview

The analysis evaluates retail sales transactions, product performance, monthly revenue trends, and customer segmentation to identify opportunities for inventory optimization and sales growth.

### Key Sales Performance Indicators

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | £8,887,208.89 |
| Total Orders | 18,532 |
| Total Units Sold | 5,152,002 |
| Average Order Value | £479.56 |

These KPIs represent valid positive sales transactions after applying the analysis filters.

## 2. Recommendations for the Inventory Team

### Recommendation 1: Prioritize High-Demand Products

**Analysis:** The Top 10 Products by Revenue chart identifies products contributing the most revenue.

**Recommended Actions:**
- Monitor inventory availability for top-performing products.
- Review historical unit sales to identify consistently high-demand items.
- Establish reorder points using sales velocity, supplier lead times, and safety stock requirements.

**Expected Benefit:** Reduced stockout risk and improved product availability.

### Recommendation 2: Improve Seasonal Inventory Planning

**Analysis:** Monthly revenue trends provide insight into changes in sales demand over time.

**Recommended Actions:**
- Review monthly and product-level demand patterns.
- Prepare inventory forecasts before historically high-demand periods.
- Avoid excessive stock purchases during periods of lower demand.

**Expected Benefit:** Improved inventory planning and reduced risk of overstocking.

### Recommendation 3: Monitor Slow-Moving Products

**Recommended Actions:**
- Identify products with consistently low unit sales.
- Review inventory turnover once stock-on-hand data is available.
- Consider targeted promotions or adjusted purchasing quantities.

**Expected Benefit:** Potential reduction in excess inventory and holding costs.

## 3. Recommendations for the Sales Team

### Recommendation 1: Improve Customer Retention

**Analysis:** RFM customer segmentation groups customers according to purchasing behavior.

**Recommended Actions:**
- Offer loyalty incentives to valuable repeat customers.
- Create re-engagement campaigns for at-risk customers.
- Personalize promotions using purchase history.

**Expected Benefit:** Increased repeat purchasing and customer lifetime value.

### Recommendation 2: Optimize Promotional Campaigns

**Recommended Actions:**
- Review previous discount sensitivity analysis.
- Identify promotions associated with stronger profit margins.
- Reduce reliance on broad discounts that may weaken profitability.

**Expected Benefit:** More profitable promotional campaigns.

### Recommendation 3: Improve Monthly Sales Performance

**Recommended Actions:**
- Monitor monthly revenue and order trends.
- Investigate periods of declining sales.
- Coordinate promotional campaigns with inventory availability.

**Expected Benefit:** Better sales planning and more consistent revenue performance.

## 4. Implementation Plan

| Priority | Team | Recommended Action | Success Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | Inventory | Monitor top-selling products | Stockout rate |
| High | Sales | Target at-risk customers | Repeat purchase rate |
| Medium | Inventory | Improve demand forecasting | Forecast accuracy |
| Medium | Sales | Optimize promotional discounts | Gross profit margin |
| Medium | Both | Coordinate sales and inventory planning | Product availability |

## 5. Conclusion

The analysis identifies opportunities to strengthen inventory planning, customer retention, and sales performance.

The Inventory team should prioritize product demand monitoring and demand forecasting, while the Sales team should focus on customer segmentation, retention campaigns, and promotional profitability.

The recommendations are proposed actions. Their financial impact should be validated using inventory records, cost data, and future campaign results.
